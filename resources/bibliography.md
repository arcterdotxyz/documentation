# Bibliography

## How far to trust these figures

Every market figure in these docs is a dated snapshot, and tokenized markets move quickly. A number can be out of date soon after it is published, so check the live source before relying on it. This list was compiled in November 2026.

Some figures come from secondary reporting and not from an issuer, a network or a primary data set. The xStocks value on Solana, Raydium's quarterly volume, the Solana uptime count and the regulatory summaries are all in that group, and the docs mark them as reported where they appear. Figures from dashboards change with their methods. Observations about mint extensions come from explorer inspection, which should be repeated before any listing.

Nothing here is legal or investment advice.
